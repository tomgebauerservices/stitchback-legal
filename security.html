Stitchback – Security

Stitchback is a Jira Cloud app that merges duplicate work items. If you found a security problem, thank you for telling us.

How to report

E-mail: security@alpscreen.de

The address is forwarded to the person who runs Stitchback. Please do not open public issues or post details in forums before we have fixed the problem. For general questions use the support page instead.

What to put in your report

  • A short description of the problem and its impact.
  • Steps to reproduce, or a proof of concept.
  • The affected area (for example a resolver, a permission check, a dependency) and the app version, if known.
  • Your contact details, and how you want to be named, if at all.

Please do not send customer content: no work item descriptions, comments, attachments, or credentials.

What to expect

  • We confirm receipt within 1 working day (Monday to Friday, German public holidays excluded).
  • We rate the severity and fix the problem within these deadlines, counted from receipt of the report:
SeverityDeadline
Critical10 days
High28 days
Medium84 days
Low175 days
  • We tell you when the fix is shipped. We agree on any public disclosure with you first.

Rules for testing

  • Test only against your own Jira site and your own data.
  • Do not access, change, or keep data that belongs to other customers or installations. If you reach such data by accident, stop and report it.
  • No denial-of-service, no spam, no social engineering, no physical attacks.

Bug bounty
